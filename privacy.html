Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information ZxTrades ("we", "us") collects when you use https://zxtrades.vercel.app and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you create an account or sign in with Google.
  • Order messages: when you tap Order, WhatsApp opens with a message containing the product, your name and your email. You send that message yourself.
  • Technical data: basic data your browser shares with the sites and services we use (such as IP address and device type).

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and access the site.
  • To process and respond to your orders and support requests.
  • To improve the site.

Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your name and email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your other Google data.

Where your data is stored

Account details are stored in your own browser on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

The site uses services that have their own privacy policies: Google (sign-in), TradingView (charts), WhatsApp (orders and support), Telegram (bot and channel), and a public exchange-rate service. Hosting is provided by Vercel.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, and you can clear your browser data to remove stored account details. To ask us about your information, message us on WhatsApp.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
